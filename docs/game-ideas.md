**Paw Steps (Simon-like):** A simple memory game where the lion shows a sequence of 4 directions (arrows) and the player must repeat them. Each round the sequence grows by one. If the player taps the wrong direction, it's game over with score = number of rounds completed. Reward is score coins (repeatable), tracked with `pawStepsBest` and increments `gamesPlayed` each completion. Fits arcade as a low-pressure memory/tap game.

**Fruit Catch (Catcher):** The lion's basket moves left/right at the bottom of the playfield as fruits fall from above. Catch ripe fruit to earn points (+5 each), avoid rotten fruit (3 hits = game over). Score = caught*5 + floor(time/1000). Reward is scaled by score (repeatable), tracked with `fruitCatchBest` and `gamesPlayed` increments per round. Reuses MangoRun's DOM rAF loop but with vertical gravity and basket input.

**Bee Stop (Precision Timing):** A swinging meter moves back and forth across a bar; press a key (Space/Enter) to stop it. Score by accuracy (perfect/good/ok) over 10 rounds. Total score = sum across rounds. Reward is total coins (repeatable), tracked with `beeStopBest` and `gamesPlayed` increments on completion. Smallest component (no collisions), easy to make accessible with reduced motion.
