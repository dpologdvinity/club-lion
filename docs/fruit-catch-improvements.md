# Fruit Catch improvement suggestions

- **More satisfying catches:** a small basket bounce, floating “+10,” and optional catch sounds. Give losing a heart equally clear feedback.
- **Smoother controls:** continuous movement while holding a key or button, plus direct pointer/touch movement with the existing buttons retained.
- **Stronger visual identity:** illustrated fruit, visibly spoiled fruit, and a lion-themed basket or catcher.
- **One interesting scoring twist:** a catch streak or occasional golden fruit could create exciting decisions and stronger replay appeal.
- **Clearer rewards:** show “You earned 24 coins” on the results screen so the connection to the rest of Club Lion is obvious.

Prioritize **controls and catch feedback**. Those should improve every moment of play before adding more mechanics.
